# 程序错位｜扫描到实物双确认
# ID: t8-case-procedure-mismatch-nested-proof-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 超尺度主体认真执行熟悉程序，扫描先揭示嵌套对象，随后同一对象实体出现完成双确认。
输入格式: 超尺度主体 + 熟悉程序 + 3个不同尺度失配 + 扫描先显示嵌套对象 + 同一对象实体出现并同框
推荐输入: 巨型纸艺飞蛾认真通过行李安检，托盘、传送带和扫描门连续出现尺度失配；屏幕先显示箱内的小纸蛾，开箱后同一只小纸蛾实体飞出，与大飞蛾同框。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 主体与程序尺度明显失配
  2. 至少三个不同失配证明
  3. 扫描先于实物揭晓
  4. 实物必须是扫描中的同一对象且两种尺度同框

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Place one visibly mis-scaled subject inside a familiar step-by-step procedure and require sincere compliance. Let each successive checkpoint expose a different physical fit failure while the subject preserves dignity. Use imaging to reveal a nested counterpart inside one bound container, then physically expose the same counterpart and hold both scales plus the main subject's reaction so the physical reveal independently confirms the scan.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Use a procedure with immediately recognizable ordered checkpoints.
- inv-02: Keep the subject sincere and restrained throughout compliance.
- inv-03: Escalate through different fit failures rather than repeated alarms.
- inv-04: Reveal the nested counterpart first through an imaging device while it remains inside the same bound container.
- inv-05: Physically expose the same counterpart and hold a visible reaction with both scales present.
Variable slots:

Failure prevention:
- An unfamiliar process cannot create clear mismatch.: Use a procedure with immediately recognizable ordered checkpoints.
- Deliberate clowning weakens the procedural contrast.: Keep the subject sincere and restrained throughout compliance.
- One repeated mismatch becomes monotonous.: Escalate through different fit failures rather than repeated alarms.
- Immediate emergence removes the scan-to-physical double-proof structure.: Reveal the nested counterpart through imaging first while it remains inside the same container.
- A screen-only ending can be read as an error, while an off-screen reveal cannot prove continuity.: Show the same counterpart physically with both scales and the main subject reaction in frame.
