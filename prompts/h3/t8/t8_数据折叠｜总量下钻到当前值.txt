# 数据折叠｜总量下钻到当前值
# ID: t8-case-fold-metrics-to-current-state-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 用一个超大总量建立尺度，展开明细记录证明总量，再折回聚合结果并切换到当前状态值，最后只用一次克制标注锁定结论。
输入格式: 超大总量 + 至少2条分类明细 + 聚合结果 + 相关里程碑 + 当前状态值 + 单次克制标注
推荐输入: 一张纸艺能源账单先显示全年总量，翻开展示三条月度记录，再折叠为季度均值；侧页露出节能里程碑，最后展开当前月数值并只盖一个确认章。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Open with one dominant total and a compact label before showing its evidence.
  2. Unfold the claim into at least two itemized records with visible category separation.
  3. Compress the detailed records into one aggregate result before changing metric family.
  4. End on the current-state value and add only one restrained annotation after the value is readable.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Open with one oversized total that calibrates scale, unfold into itemized records that prove the claim, refold those records into an aggregate result, pivot through one intermediate milestone to a current-state value, then use one restrained manual annotation to lock the conclusion.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Open with one dominant total and a compact label before showing its evidence.
- inv-02: Unfold the claim into at least two itemized records with visible category separation.
- inv-03: Compress the detailed records into one aggregate result before changing metric family.
- inv-04: Use one physically legible panel or carrier change to reveal an intermediate milestone from a different but related metric family.
- inv-05: End on the current-state value and add only one restrained annotation after the value is readable.
Variable slots:
- headline_total: One original, truthful or explicitly fictional dominant total with a short label.
- record_set: At least two distinct records whose categories visibly support the headline.
- aggregate_result: One summary value derived from the record set.
- milestone_metric: One related intermediate milestone shown through a physical carrier change.
- current_state: One final current value, not a duplicate of the opening total.
- material_system: An original medium whose folds, slides, hinges or layer changes explain information transitions.
- annotation: One brief non-brand manual mark added only after the final value is readable.
- sound_family: Original tactile cues tied to each information-state change.
Failure prevention:
- The opening contains several equal-sized figures.: Choose one dominant total and one compact label.
- The detail state is decorative rather than evidentiary.: Give each record a distinct category and a visible relation to the headline.
- The sequence skips the aggregate summary.: Compress records into one result before switching metric family.
- The milestone arrives through an arbitrary cut.: Use one hinge, fold, slide or layer change to carry the pivot.
- The final annotation obscures or repeats the value.: Wait until the current value is readable, then add one restrained mark.
