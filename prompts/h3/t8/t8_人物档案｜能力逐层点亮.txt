# 人物档案｜能力逐层点亮
# ID: t8-case-layered-dossier-activation-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 固定档案界面不换主体，按身份、诊断、能力、工具和待命状态逐层激活。
输入格式: 稳定人物 + 固定档案界面 + 身份→诊断→能力→工具→待命状态
推荐输入: 深海潜水员始终位于固定档案框：先显示身份轮廓，再扫描呼吸系统，随后点亮耐压能力、展开机械工具，最后进入待命姿态。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 人物身份稳定
  2. 档案框架固定
  3. 信息层级按功能递进
  4. 终态是可执行的待命状态

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Hold one subject and one modular information grid completely stable while a dossier boots from absence to completeness in a strict hierarchy. Each phase activates a new semantic layer—identity, role, diagnostics, capability, equipment and status—while the subject performs only micro-motion. Information order, not camera travel, creates retention; subject, domain, panel geometry, palette and labels are variable.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Begin with a partially hidden subject inside a fixed modular grid.
- inv-02: Activate information layers in a semantic hierarchy from identity to capability.
- inv-03: Keep subject pose and grid geometry stable while module states change.
- inv-04: Use one restrained accent color to indicate activation and completion.
- inv-05: End with one global active-state pulse after all key modules are complete.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location.
- escalation_variable: One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages.
- performance_rule: One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries.
- payoff: One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain.
- sound_family: A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change.
Failure prevention:
- Every panel appears simultaneously.: Stage identity, diagnostics, capabilities and equipment in separate phases.
- The camera or character constantly moves.: Lock geometry and allow only breathing, eye or light micro-motion.
- Generated small text becomes the focus.: Use large trusted labels or abstract icons and bars; keep microcopy secondary.
Do not copy from the source: named ZENITH cybernetic operative and exact face; cyan-black tactical dossier with source panel arrangement; cybernetic arm diagnostic image and source stat values; source headings, labels, iconography and final slogan
