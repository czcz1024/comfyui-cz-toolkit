# 类型预告｜同一主角跨场景升级到片名
# ID: t8-case-protagonist-genre-escalation-trailer-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 以同一核心人物和少数关系锚点贯穿多场景预告：先建立关系，再扩展城市活动与私人压力，随后把冲突升级到公共危险和大尺度奇观，最终用独立片名卡封口；不依赖未转写对白意义。
输入格式: 固定主角与关系锚点 + 公共世界扩展 + 私人脆弱阶段 + 物理危险升级 + 独立原创片名终卡
推荐输入: 毛毡植物学家预告从温室友情、河上市集远行与种子库危机，升级到机械果园救援和发光花粉爆发，最后切到独立的原创无字叶片徽记。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Introduce one protagonist and one relationship anchor before the location count expands.
  2. Return to the protagonist while broadening public settings and group size.
  3. Escalate from interpersonal pressure to visible public physical danger.
  4. Separate the maximum spectacle from a held, original title lockup.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
A short trailer can escalate genre without dialogue evidence by repeatedly returning to one protagonist while broadening the world in five causal registers: relationship, public activity, private vulnerability, physical danger and maximum spectacle. A separate final title card closes the promise; recurring faces and objects, not unverified speech meaning, carry continuity.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Introduce one protagonist and one relationship anchor before the location count expands.
- inv-02: Return to the protagonist while broadening public settings and group size.
- inv-03: Insert a quieter private or clinical vulnerability phase before action escalation.
- inv-04: Escalate from interpersonal pressure to visible public physical danger.
- inv-05: Separate the maximum spectacle from a held, original title lockup.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- Unrelated opening faces create a generic clip reel.: Introduce one protagonist and one relationship anchor before the location count expands.
- Pure location montage loses narrative ownership.: Return to the protagonist while broadening public settings and group size.
- Constant action leaves no emotional pressure change.: Insert a quieter private or clinical vulnerability phase before action escalation.
- An arbitrary explosion has no dramatic ladder.: Escalate from interpersonal pressure to visible public physical danger.
- No title closure makes the montage feel like an excerpt.: Separate the maximum spectacle from a held, original title lockup.
