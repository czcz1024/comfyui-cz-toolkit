# 计划对照｜固定分镜条见证执行升级
# ID: t8-case-persistent-plan-strip-execution-escalation-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 让一条静态计划板持续可读，执行区按计划顺序逐步完成动作，并用不断累积的现场变化证明执行正在推进；最后让实际结果与计划末项形成可读闭合。
输入格式: 持续可读的计划板 + 独立执行区 + 计划顺序推进 + 至少三项可见状态变化 + 计划与终态同框
推荐输入: 原创极地微缩观测站内，左侧五格黄铜任务计划条始终完整可读，右侧较大执行区建立陶瓷勘测自动机、三座冰晶站、倾斜透镜和折叠信标；自动机支撑身体并把透镜旋到正确轴线，冰面出现一条细琥珀反射；左侧计划条仍固定；信标发出一道稳定蓝白光柱并与计划条最后一格对齐；自动机、三座完成站和完整计划条同框停留

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Establish one persistent planning artifact and one separate execution field in the opening.
  2. Advance the execution field while keeping the planning artifact unchanged and readable.
  3. Accumulate at least two further state changes while preserving subject identity, route and plan visibility.
  4. Hold a terminal result with the persistent plan still visible for direct comparison.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Keep one compact planning artifact continuously visible and spatially stable while a larger execution field performs the planned stages in order. Let the execution accumulate real state changes and increasing consequence while the plan remains unchanged as an audit reference, then hold a terminal result that can be compared directly with the plan's final duty.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish one persistent planning artifact and one separate execution field in the opening.
- inv-02: Advance the execution field while keeping the planning artifact unchanged and readable.
- inv-03: Convert one planned action into a visible physical consequence inside the execution field.
- inv-04: Accumulate at least two further state changes while preserving subject identity, route and plan visibility.
- inv-05: Hold a terminal result with the persistent plan still visible for direct comparison.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- A hidden or late plan turns the sequence into ordinary action footage.: Establish one persistent planning artifact and one separate execution field in the opening.
- Changing both layers together destroys the audit relationship.: Advance the execution field while keeping the planning artifact unchanged and readable.
- Decorative motion without consequence cannot establish execution.: Convert one planned action into a visible physical consequence inside the execution field.
- Disconnected spectacle makes plan and result impossible to compare.: Accumulate at least two further state changes while preserving subject identity, route and plan visibility.
- Removing the plan before the result abandons the defining proof.: Hold a terminal result with the persistent plan still visible for direct comparison.
