# 重复复位｜多场景小灾难逐次升级
# ID: t8-case-repeat-reset-escalating-mishap-anthology-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 同一角色关系反复执行普通准备、主动动作、可见后果、见证者反应和场景复位；每轮更换物理问题并提高后果强度。
输入格式: 固定角色关系 + 三轮准备/动作/后果/反应 + 场景复位 + 后果逐轮升级 + 最大结果同框
推荐输入: 原创定格工坊第一幕，铜质学徒机器人拉动松弹簧，垫圈在同一盏绿色监督灯前散开；磁刷把颜料珠拉成扩大轨迹，绿灯清楚后仰，后果大于第一幕；铜机器人、倾斜绿灯与最大齿轮场同框停住，不加入对白或修复结果

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Keep the same active-role and witness relationship readable across every reset.
  2. Establish an ordinary object and action before each mishap.
  3. Increase consequence scale or spatial reach across rounds.
  4. Hold both roles and the largest consequence together.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Repeat a compact vignette grammar across different spaces: ordinary setup, one autonomous action by the same active role, one visible material consequence, one reaction by the recurring witness, then a clean reset. Change the physical problem each round and increase consequence scale before a held final reaction.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Keep the same active-role and witness relationship readable across every reset.
- inv-02: Establish an ordinary object and action before each mishap.
- inv-03: Use a different material consequence in each round.
- inv-04: Increase consequence scale or spatial reach across rounds.
- inv-05: Hold both roles and the largest consequence together.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- Changing both roles turns the sequence into unrelated clips.: Keep the same active-role and witness relationship readable across every reset.
- Opening on a mess removes responsibility and timing.: Establish an ordinary object and action before each mishap.
- Repeating one mishap adds no proof.: Use a different material consequence in each round.
- Random intensity without a ladder feels arbitrary.: Increase consequence scale or spatial reach across rounds.
- Cutting before the reaction loses the payoff.: Hold both roles and the largest consequence together.
