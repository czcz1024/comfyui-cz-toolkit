# 表演让位于环境｜近脸到全景的连续退镜
# ID: t8-case-performance-environment-pullback-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 从身份清楚的近脸表演开始，表演不中断，单次连续退镜把视觉主导权逐步从面部交给身体，再交给响应中的环境；结尾人物仍可辨，但环境成为主角。
输入格式: 近脸表演基准 + 连续后退镜头 + 逐层环境参照 + 人物动作不中断 + 人景关系全景终态
推荐输入: 原创风场舞者从面部近景开始持续表演，镜头连续后退，依次揭示服装、步道、海崖与天空光带，最后人景同框。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Open close enough to establish one stable performer identity.
  2. Keep one continuous performance thread through the camera retreat.
  3. Transfer visual dominance through camera distance rather than a subject transformation.
  4. End wide with both performer and responsive environment readable.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Begin in an identity-close performance frame, keep the performance continuous while one unbroken pullback gradually transfers visual dominance from face to body and then to a responsive environment, and end with the performer still readable inside the now-dominant wide setting.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Open close enough to establish one stable performer identity.
- inv-02: Keep one continuous performance thread through the camera retreat.
- inv-03: Reveal the first environmental response before the frame becomes wide.
- inv-04: Transfer visual dominance through camera distance rather than a subject transformation.
- inv-05: End wide with both performer and responsive environment readable.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity.
- setting: A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location.
- causal_carrier: One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain.
- state_change: Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage.
- camera_grammar: A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change.
Failure prevention:
- Opening wide makes the performer secondary too early.: Open close enough to establish one stable performer identity.
- Stopping performance breaks the causal link.: Keep one continuous performance thread through the camera retreat.
- A sudden final background replacement feels unrelated.: Reveal the first environmental response before the frame becomes wide.
- Changing the performer instead of the frame changes the mechanism.: Transfer visual dominance through camera distance rather than a subject transformation.
- A landscape-only ending discards the opening anchor.: End wide with both performer and responsive environment readable.
