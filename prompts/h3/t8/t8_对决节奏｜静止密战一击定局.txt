# 对决节奏｜静止密战一击定局
# ID: t8-case-calm-dense-decisive-delay-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 先安静建立，进入连续密集交换，再用一次决定性干预、短暂假稳定和延迟结果终结。
输入格式: 两个主体/系统 + 可读静止 + 连续密集交换 + 一次决定性干预 + 短暂假稳定 + 延迟结果
推荐输入: 两组动力雕塑先静止对峙，随后连续交换高速磁片；其中一组只做一次反向脉冲，场面短暂恢复平静，数秒后对方结构延迟塌落。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 安静建立可读
  2. 中段交换连续且高密
  3. 决定性干预只有一次
  4. 假稳定后才出现不可逆结果

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Organize a two-agent confrontation into three readable states: measured stillness, one continuous dense exchange, and a single decisive intervention. After the intervention, preserve one short false-stability beat before an irreversible physical consequence confirms the result; end on the initiator's controlled state rather than another attack.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Begin with readable stillness and a clear relation between both agents.
- inv-02: Treat the middle as one continuous exchange rather than unrelated highlights.
- inv-03: Use only one identifiable state-changing intervention.
- inv-04: Hold a short false-stability beat after intervention.
- inv-05: Confirm the outcome through irreversible physical state while the initiator stays controlled.

Failure prevention:
