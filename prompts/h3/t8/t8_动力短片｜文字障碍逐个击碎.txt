# 动力短片｜文字障碍逐个击碎
# ID: t8-case-kinetic-text-obstacle-break-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 低饱和世界里，短词成为路线上的实体障碍，主角通过前进动作逐个击碎，每次冲击恢复一部分颜色，最后用一个原创短句命名新状态。
输入格式: 受抑制环境基线 + 2个短词实体障碍 + 主角前进击碎 + 每次恢复环境状态 + 一个原创结尾短句
推荐输入: 配送无人机穿过灰色物流隧道，依次撞碎实体词 DELAY 与 HOLD，每次冲击恢复一段路线灯，最后停入装载区并出现短句 ROUTE CLEAR。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 开场先建立受抑制环境状态
  2. 短词必须占据真实路线空间
  3. 障碍只能由主角前进动作击碎
  4. 每次击碎恢复可见环境状态
  5. 结尾只用一个原创短句命名结果

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Begin in a visually suppressed world, bind one short word or phrase to a physical obstacle in the subject's route, and require the subject's forward action to break that obstacle rather than merely passing it. Each break restores a visible part of the world; finish with one original concise statement that names the achieved state, not the source slogan.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Begin with one visibly suppressed environmental state.
- inv-02: Turn a short readable word into a real obstacle on the route.
- inv-03: Require forward subject action to break each obstacle.
- inv-04: Make each break restore a visible part of the environment.
- inv-05: End with one original concise statement describing the achieved state.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- Without a baseline, color restoration has no consequence.: Begin with one visibly suppressed environmental state.
- Floating captions do not participate in causality.: Turn a short readable word into a real obstacle on the route.
- Passive explosions detach the change from the protagonist.: Require forward subject action to break each obstacle.
- If nothing changes, repeated breaks feel interchangeable.: Make each break restore a visible part of the environment.
- A long motivational paragraph overwhelms the visual result.: End with one original concise statement describing the achieved state.
