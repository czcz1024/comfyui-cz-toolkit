# 舞台表演｜远景起势到倒地复起
# ID: t8-case-performance-fall-rise-arc-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 单人表演先用大远景建立身体与空间，再推进近景表达、完成一次受控倒地和俯拍停留，最后在独立光束中复起。
输入格式: 单人表演者 + 巨大空场 + 远景起势 + 近景表达 + 一次受控倒地 + 俯拍确认 + 连续复起
推荐输入: 打击乐舞者独自在废弃天文台起舞，从大远景轨迹进入近景手部节奏，完成一次膝盖先着地的螺旋倒地，俯拍停留后在开启的穹顶光束中连续起身。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 开场先证明全身与巨大场地尺度
  2. 近景必须晚于动作语言建立
  3. 全片只有一次决定性下降
  4. 用俯拍或地面机位证明落地
  5. 结尾连续复起且不回到开场构图

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Build a solo performance through a scale-changing camera arc: establish the whole body in a large empty venue, advance to close expressive proof, let the body descend into one floor-level low point, then restore vertical energy in a final isolated light. The reusable template excludes any storyboard or prompt-demo overlay and depends on the visible fall-to-rise performance logic.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Open with full-body scale inside a visibly larger venue.
- inv-02: Move closer only after the body's initial movement language is established.
- inv-03: Use one controlled descent as the single decisive low point.
- inv-04: Hold the low point with a camera relation that proves floor contact and body geometry.
- inv-05: End upright in one isolated light, without recreating the opening framing.

Failure prevention:
- Opening close removes the scale contrast needed later.: Open with full-body scale inside a visibly larger venue.
- Immediate close coverage turns the piece into a generic portrait.: Move closer only after the body's initial movement language is established.
- Repeated falls become choreography noise rather than structure.: Use one controlled descent as the single decisive low point.
- A hidden landing weakens physical causality.: Hold the low point with a camera relation that proves floor contact and body geometry.
- Returning to the exact opening makes the arc feel reset rather than resolved.: End upright in one isolated light, without recreating the opening framing.
