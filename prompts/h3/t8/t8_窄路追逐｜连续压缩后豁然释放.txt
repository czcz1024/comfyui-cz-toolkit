# 窄路追逐｜连续压缩后豁然释放
# ID: t8-case-constricted-route-open-release-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 让高速载具与镜头始终绑定在同一狭窄路线，连续用贴墙、拱洞、垂落物与隧道压缩可用空间；最后只经过一次最窄出口，突然释放到无遮挡的大尺度环境。
输入格式: 同一载具与路线轴 + 至少三种逐步收窄障碍 + 唯一最窄门槛 + 开阔空间释放
推荐输入: 原创水下珊瑚维护沟内，一艘琥珀勘测潜航器沿玄武岩槽高速进入，首个窄段前完成载具锁定；垂落海藻压低上方空间，两只氧化阀环横跨路线旋转，潜航器连续穿过中心；潜航器只跨越一次出口，进入没有近障碍的巨大生物发光水盆上方，镜头停下来证明开阔尺度

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Establish one fast carrier, forward route and coupled camera before the first hard constriction.
  2. Reduce usable clearance progressively with side, overhead and route obstacles.
  3. Use one final tunnel or aperture as the narrowest visible compression.
  4. Cross once into an obstacle-free large environment and let the camera widen or slow.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Lock a fast carrier and its camera to one continuous constrained route. Escalate by reducing clearance from different sides while preserving forward direction and recurring route geometry. Make one final tunnel or aperture the tightest compression, then cross it exactly once into a materially wider environment where near obstacles disappear and the camera finally slows enough to prove the release.
