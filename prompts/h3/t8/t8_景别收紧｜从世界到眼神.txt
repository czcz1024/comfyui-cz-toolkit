# 景别收紧｜从世界到眼神

用途: 景别从宏大环境逐步收紧，按人物、局部发现、自有装置和眼神反应形成证据漏斗。
输入格式: 宏大环境 + 稳定人物/双人 + 地面或局部发现 + 人物自有装置 + 最后近景反应
推荐输入: 轨道维修舱先展示宏大地球背景，两名工程师保持稳定位置；镜头收紧到地面金属垫片，再到其中一人的腕部检测器，最后停在两人克制交换的眼神。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 景别持续收紧
  2. 人物空间关系稳定
  3. 证据从环境转到局部再到装置
  4. 最后用近景反应闭合

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Begin with monumental environment scale and a tiny stable subject pair. Contract framing in discrete evidence steps—paired medium, full-body investigation, object inspection, subject-owned mechanism insert and eye-level reaction—so every scale change answers one question and raises a more personal one. Preserve route, axis and identity, keep performance restrained, and stop at the facial endpoint instead of adding another action branch.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish world scale, route and the stable subject group in an extreme or very wide opening.
- inv-02: Contract framing through ordered information layers rather than random beauty shots.
- inv-03: Move the evidence from the shared environment to a local object and then to a mechanism owned by the lead subject.
- inv-04: Preserve the same route axis, subject identities and wardrobe or material markers across every scale change.
- inv-05: End on one restrained face or sensor response immediately after the closest mechanism proof.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject_group: One stable person, pair or machine group whose identity remains continuous.
- monumental_setting: A setting with a readable route and strong wide-scale geography.
- local_discovery: One small external object or state that justifies stopping.
- owned_mechanism: One tool, garment mechanism or device physically associated with the lead subject.
- scale_ladder: A deliberate wide-to-close information order in which each scale reveals new evidence.
- performance_rule: Restrained testing and recognition rather than exaggerated reaction.
- final_response: One face, lens or status response that closes the sequence.
Failure prevention:
- Opening close removes the scale contrast and makes later inserts feel arbitrary.: Begin with a route-readable monumental wide and stable subject group.
- Repeated similar coverage reveals no new question and produces a flat montage.: Assign one new evidence job to every scale contraction.
- Unrelated props do not create an inward causal progression.: Link the external discovery and subject-owned mechanism through one visible state.
- Continuity drift makes the scale ladder look like disconnected footage.: Lock route axis, identity and material markers across all shots.
- A new action after the reaction restarts the sequence and weakens the funnel closure.: Hold the final face or sensor response as the endpoint.
