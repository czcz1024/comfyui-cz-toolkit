# 环境前沿｜局部点燃到全域占据
# ID: t8-case-environmental-front-area-escalation-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 在同一地理中用一个可追踪的环境前沿逐阶段扩张：局部起点、连续蔓延、跨越地标、遮蔽更多空间，最终用广角证明场地状态已整体改变；不依赖来源中的战斗身份或火焰表面。
输入格式: 有边界环境 + 单一连续变化前沿 + 至少2个固定地标 + 占据面积逐级扩大 + 终态广角对照
推荐输入: 圆形水族实验室中，掌大蓝色藻团从进水口连续扩张，依次越过三座编号珊瑚门；最终俯拍证明大部分水槽已改变，同时保留一个清水口袋对照。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Establish a bounded environment, an untouched region and one localized starting front.
  2. Grow one contiguous front instead of spawning unrelated effects everywhere.
  3. Increase occupied area and material state, not just brightness.
  4. Hold a final wide comparison showing transformed and residual untouched regions.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Environmental escalation is legible when one contiguous front grows through a fixed geography and each phase occupies a larger measurable area. Static anchors must remain visible long enough to prove propagation, and the final wide state should show that the environment—not merely exposure or color grading—has materially changed.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish a bounded environment, an untouched region and one localized starting front.
- inv-02: Grow one contiguous front instead of spawning unrelated effects everywhere.
- inv-03: Cross at least two fixed spatial anchors in order.
- inv-04: Increase occupied area and material state, not just brightness.
- inv-05: Hold a final wide comparison showing transformed and residual untouched regions.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- Opening on a fully transformed field removes progression.: Establish a bounded environment, an untouched region and one localized starting front.
- Random flashes create spectacle without a readable process.: Grow one contiguous front instead of spawning unrelated effects everywhere.
- Without anchors, apparent growth may be only camera movement.: Cross at least two fixed spatial anchors in order.
- Exposure shifts can imitate escalation without propagation.: Increase occupied area and material state, not just brightness.
- A tight climax cannot verify site-wide change.: Hold a final wide comparison showing transformed and residual untouched regions.
Do not copy from the source: dark battlefield terrain; large silhouetted creature; orange fire waves; source ignition layout; source battle and flame climax
