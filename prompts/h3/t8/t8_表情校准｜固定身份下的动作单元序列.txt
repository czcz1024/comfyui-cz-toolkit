# 表情校准｜固定身份下的动作单元序列
# ID: t8-case-facial-action-unit-calibration-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 锁定人物、光线和视角，只让眼睑、眉部、下颌、视线和唇形等局部动作单元分段变化；中间用微小复位防止表情混成连续表演，末段收紧景别检验身份与细节仍稳定。
输入格式: 单一稳定人像 + 固定光线背景视角 + 至少4组分离面部动作 + 1次微小复位 + 后段细节近景
推荐输入: 年轻陶瓷快递员在冷色医疗亭中保持同一身份与正面机位，依次展示单侧微笑、闭眼、抬眉、侧视和圆唇；中间短暂回中性，最后只推进到面罩密封近景。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Lock one face, background, lighting direction and camera axis before any expression change.
  2. Separate the expression into readable local units rather than morphing the whole face continuously.
  3. Keep head motion subordinate to eye, brow, mouth and cheek evidence.
  4. Use a late closer crop only after identity has been repeatedly proven.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
A facial calibration sequence becomes reusable when identity, lighting, background and camera axis stay fixed while distinct facial action units change one group at a time. Brief micro-resets preserve comparability, and a late controlled crop can test fine identity stability without turning the sequence into a narrative performance.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Lock one face, background, lighting direction and camera axis before any expression change.
- inv-02: Separate the expression into readable local units rather than morphing the whole face continuously.
- inv-03: Insert at least one partial reset toward the baseline between larger changes.
- inv-04: Keep head motion subordinate to eye, brow, mouth and cheek evidence.
- inv-05: Use a late closer crop only after identity has been repeatedly proven.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- Changing the portrait setup makes action units impossible to compare.: Lock one face, background, lighting direction and camera axis before any expression change.
- A blended emotional performance hides which feature changed.: Separate the expression into readable local units rather than morphing the whole face continuously.
- Without a reset, changes can drift into a different face.: Insert at least one partial reset toward the baseline between larger changes.
- Large head turns replace facial proof with pose variation.: Keep head motion subordinate to eye, brow, mouth and cheek evidence.
- Opening in extreme close-up removes the identity baseline.: Use a late closer crop only after identity has been repeatedly proven.
