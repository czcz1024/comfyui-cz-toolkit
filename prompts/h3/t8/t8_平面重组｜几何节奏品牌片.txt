# 平面重组｜几何节奏品牌片
# ID: t8-case-flat-geometry-reconstruction-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 稳定剪影与几何母题被反复拆解重组，在高密阶段后短暂停顿并收束为既有图形族。
输入格式: 单一几何母题 + 稳定剪影 + 反复拆解重组 + 高密阶段 + 短暂停顿 + 既有图形收束
推荐输入: 以珊瑚螺旋为唯一母题，稳定潜航器剪影贯穿；螺旋反复拆成声呐弧线和路线图，在高密数据阶段后短暂停顿，最终重组为同一图形族的徽记。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 只有一个几何母题
  2. 主体剪影稳定
  3. 拆解与重组多次发生
  4. 高密后留短暂停顿并用既有图形收束

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Build a flat poster world around one dominant geometric motif and one stable protagonist silhouette. Repeatedly fracture and reconstruct the layout through hard-edged crops, diagrams, negative space and key states; reserve continuous physical action, then slow briefly for a contrastive operational tableau and resolve all graphic families into one closing system. Motif, palette, subject class, icon family, tempo, contrast state and final graphic outcome are variable.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish one dominant geometric motif and protagonist silhouette immediately.
- inv-02: Keep all space flat and poster-like while graphic planes transform.
- inv-03: Use key states and symbolic environmental inserts instead of continuous combat.
- inv-04: Peak information density before a short contrastive operational tableau.
- inv-05: Resolve the established graphic families into one original closing system.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location.
- escalation_variable: One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages.
- performance_rule: One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries.
- payoff: One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain.
- sound_family: A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change.
Failure prevention:
- The clip becomes realistic 3D scenery.: Use flat planes, hard shadows, graphic crops and no volumetric perspective.
- Characters perform continuous combat.: Reduce movement to a few iconic key poses and let layout transitions carry energy.
- The final mark introduces unrelated graphics.: Build it only from motif, grid and icon families already established.
Do not copy from the source: giant purple lunar eclipse and lunar phase diagrams; black-haired woman with massive katana; gray-haired villain in a wide-brim black hat; source purple-black-silver palette, raven inserts and exact astrology UI
