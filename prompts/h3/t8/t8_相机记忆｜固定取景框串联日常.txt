# 相机记忆｜固定取景框串联日常
# ID: t8-case-recorder-overlay-memory-route-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 一个固定相机取景界面贯穿多处日常空间，同一人物每到一处完成一个小动作，最后用手掌触镜物理结束记录。
输入格式: 同一人物 + 固定相机界面 + 至少3处连续路线空间 + 每处一个小动作 + 一个明确阈值过渡 + 触镜结束
推荐输入: 年长博物馆修复师在固定口袋相机界面中穿过档案室、修复室、楼梯和雕塑庭院，每处完成一个小动作，最后在屋顶用戴手套的手掌完全盖住镜头。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 同一原创相机界面全程不变
  2. 同一人物穿过至少三处连续空间
  3. 每处空间有一个小动作
  4. 至少一次阈值或楼梯过渡
  5. 结尾触镜物理结束记录

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Lock one recorder-style viewing interface over the entire film, then let one subject move through a compact route of clearly different everyday places. Each place contributes one small human action and one spatial clue; finish with a direct gesture into the lens so the fixed interface feels like a remembered recording rather than decorative graphics.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Keep one original recorder interface visually stable for the whole sequence.
- inv-02: Keep one subject and one observer relationship continuous across at least three places.
- inv-03: Give each place one small readable action rather than a posed beauty shot.
- inv-04: Use one physical threshold or travel action between interior and exterior scale.
- inv-05: End with a direct lens interaction that physically closes the recording.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical, informational or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- If the interface changes every shot, it becomes decorative motion graphics.: Keep one original recorder interface visually stable for the whole sequence.
- Unrelated people and places become a travel montage.: Keep one subject and one observer relationship continuous across at least three places.
- Pure posing makes the interface feel like a filter.: Give each place one small readable action rather than a posed beauty shot.
- Abrupt location swaps lose the feeling of an outing.: Use one physical threshold or travel action between interior and exterior scale.
- A fade without interaction does not complete the observer relationship.: End with a direct lens interaction that physically closes the recording.
