# 微缩闯关｜同一材质连续变形
# ID: t8-case-material-role-traversal-ladder-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 让一个微型主体穿越同一巨型地形，地形依次变成路面、阻力、载体和弹性过渡，最后由一个外部尺度工具只进入一次完成跨越。
输入格式: 微型主体 + 单一巨型地形 + 同一材质至少3种连续物理角色 + 一次晚段外部桥梁 + 恢复自主的稳定结尾
推荐输入: 微型温室信使横穿一片巨型蜡质叶：叶面从路脊变成湿滑露水面、移动叶脉和弹性纤维；最后一支全尺寸玻璃滴管只进入一次成为桥，信使抵达种子标记旁稳定站立。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 开场先证明主体与地形尺度
  2. 同一材质至少承担三种连续物理角色
  3. 每次解决动作必须引出下一个材质状态
  4. 外部尺度工具只在后段进入一次
  5. 结尾主体恢复自主稳定状态

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Establish one tiny protagonist crossing a single oversized terrain, then make that same substrate change physical role in a causal ladder: reliable footing becomes resistance, resistance becomes an improvised carrier, and the carrier becomes an elastic transition. Only after the protagonist has attempted those states, introduce one full-scale tool exactly once as a final bridge. End with the protagonist back in a stable self-possessed pose so scale assistance resolves the route without erasing agency.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Prove the protagonist-to-terrain scale relation in the opening before the first obstacle.
- inv-02: Make one continuous substrate perform at least three visibly different physical roles in causal order.
- inv-03: Let each attempted solution expose the next material state rather than resetting the route.
- inv-04: Use one late external-scale tool exactly once as a bridge, not as the opening scale reveal.
- inv-05: End on a stable pose that visibly restores agency after the external bridge.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original, stable subject or group with a readable role.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- escalation_variable: One causal variable that changes through visible stages.
- performance_rule: A restrained acting rule that keeps the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A clean final physical or social consequence.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting state changes.
Failure prevention:
- Without early scale proof, later material deformation reads as arbitrary VFX.: Prove the protagonist-to-terrain scale relation in the opening before the first obstacle.
- If every beat uses a new prop, the sequence becomes a montage instead of one evolving material problem.: Make one continuous substrate perform at least three visibly different physical roles in causal order.
- Independent gags remove the sense that the traveler is learning and adapting.: Let each attempted solution expose the next material state rather than resetting the route.
- An early or repeated giant tool steals agency and collapses the material traversal into handling.: Use one late external-scale tool exactly once as a bridge, not as the opening scale reveal.
- Ending in midair or on the tool leaves the causal result unresolved.: End on a stable pose that visibly restores agency after the external bridge.
