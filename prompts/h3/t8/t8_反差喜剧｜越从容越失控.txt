# 反差喜剧｜越从容越失控
# ID: t8-case-deadpan-chain-failure-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 正式场合中连锁失控不断升级，但主体始终镇定，反差本身构成笑点。
输入格式: 正式场合 + 附着负担 + 至少2次连锁后果 + 可见失效 + 主体始终从容
推荐输入: 糕点师在颁奖展示中背着巨大的糖艺帆，帆先扫倒装饰塔，再扯翻展示布，最后糖船断裂；他始终面带微笑完成鞠躬。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 正式场合与沉着主体
  2. 负担始终附着主体
  3. 至少两次因果连锁
  4. 失效可见但主体不慌

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Place an impeccably controlled subject inside a rigid public ritual, introduce one initially manageable physical liability, escalate its collateral effects in countable steps, then expose a visible failure while the subject preserves exactly the same confident performance rule. The mismatch between increasing chaos and unchanged poise creates the payoff; casting, setting, hazard, costume, palette and final consequence are variable.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish a formal ritual with a clear success posture before disruption begins.
- inv-02: Introduce one physical liability attached to the subject and keep its cause visible.
- inv-03: Escalate collateral consequences at least twice while the subject maintains the same poise.
- inv-04: Show one irreversible visible failure only after collateral has peaked.
- inv-05: End on confident continuation rather than explanation or embarrassment.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable.
- setting: A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location.
- escalation_variable: One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages.
- performance_rule: One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible.
- camera_grammar: A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries.
- payoff: One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain.
- sound_family: A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change.
Failure prevention:
- Several unrelated accidents happen at once.: Use one attached liability and show how each consequence follows from it.
- The subject reacts with panic before the visible failure.: Lock one confident expression and posture until the final hold.
- The new version simply changes the runway animal.: Change ritual, setting, hazard physics, camera grammar, costume and payoff together.
