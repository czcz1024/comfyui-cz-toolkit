# 固定机位｜从线稿生成成品
# ID: t8c002-fixed-composition-medium-ladder
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 锁定构图，用不可逆的制作阶段让同一对象从草图逐步成为成品。
输入格式: 对象 + 固定构图 + 4个不可逆制作阶段 + 终态小动作
推荐输入: 固定正面构图中的纸艺灯塔：铅笔轮廓、纸片立体搭建、色彩与纹理完成、内部灯光点亮，最后纸浪轻轻摆动。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 构图锁定
  2. 同一对象连续成长
  3. 至少四个不可逆阶段
  4. 终态有轻微活化动作

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
A fixed composition turns creation into a readable causal ladder: low-commitment guides establish geometry, committed structure locks it, material finish adds specificity, contextual details certify completion, and one restrained in-medium motion supplies the final surprise.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Lock the camera and reserve stable spatial zones before any marks appear.
- inv-02: Progress from low-commitment guides to committed structure to material colour or finish.
- inv-03: Delay secondary labels, studies or context until the hero form is already readable.
- inv-04: End with one subtle motion that remains faithful to the finished medium while the support stays inert.
Variable slots:
- support: One stable physical or graphic surface with reserved hero and context zones.
- hero_subject: A single dominant form readable first as guides, then structure, then finish.
- representation_ladder: Three visibly distinct commitment stages appropriate to the chosen medium.
- context_details: Two to four secondary marks or diagrams that arrive only after the hero reads.
- finish_palette: A restrained palette that clarifies material stages.
- living_idle: One small loop or response that does not leave or volumize the surface.
- sound_ladder: Material sounds change with each process stage, followed by one payoff accent.
Failure prevention:
- All layers appear together as a generic dissolve.: Give each representation stage a distinct material behavior and complete one milestone before the next begins.
- The support or camera drifts during creation.: Lock framing, shadows, surface texture and prior marks; permit motion only inside active drawing regions.
- The final animation breaks into three dimensions.: Restrict the payoff to one flat, local, low-amplitude motion that preserves the medium boundary.
