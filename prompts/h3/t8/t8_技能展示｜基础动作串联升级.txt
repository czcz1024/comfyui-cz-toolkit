# 技能展示｜基础动作串联升级
# ID: t8-case-base-loop-skill-ladder-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 持续基础动作连接逐级高级技能，每个机位只证明一种运动，峰值后回到基础循环。
输入格式: 持续基础动作 + 3种高级技能 + 每次不同功能机位 + 节奏/接触声连续 + 回到基础动作
推荐输入: 检修无人机保持稳定巡航基础动作，依次完成窄缝侧飞、旋转扫描和高速对接；每段用不同机位证明技能，峰值后回到同一巡航节奏。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. 基础动作反复返回
  2. 至少三项技能逐级变难
  3. 每个机位只承担一种证明任务
  4. 峰值后回到基础循环

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Start inside one continuous base motion and make it the mandatory entry and exit for every distinct skill. Increase difficulty across countable stages, assign each camera angle a verification purpose such as contact, clearance, rotation or recovery, combine learned principles near the end, then perform one peak variation and visibly return to the unchanged base loop.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Begin mid-loop and keep the base motion active between every skill.
- inv-02: Every skill must depart from and visibly return to the loop.
- inv-03: Increase difficulty through distinct movement properties.
- inv-04: Give each camera angle one proof job.
- inv-05: After the peak, return to the original loop without stopping.
