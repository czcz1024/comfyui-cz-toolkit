# 双角色职责｜独立建立后共同完成
# ID: t8-case-independent-roles-to-shared-result-v1
# 来源: T8 非官方案例模板

用途: 先分别用工具、局部动作和中间结果建立两个角色的独立职责，随后让双方在一个共享接口产生不可替代的依赖，最终以缺少任一方都无法完成的可见成果收束。
输入格式: 角色A工具/操作/中间结果 + 角色B不同工具/操作/中间结果 + 共享接口 + 双贡献成果
推荐输入: 铜腿土壤采样自动机完成矿物路径，玻璃泵站自动机独立建立水压；两者在矩形混合阀会合，缺一不可地启动滴灌并让幼苗成排抬起。

必须实现的锚点:
  1. Establish Character A through one stable tool, local operation and visible intermediate result.
  2. Keep Character A's identity and result legible while its local operation completes.
  3. Bring both roles to one shared interface where each contribution is visibly required.
  4. Hold a concrete joint result while preserving both identities, tools and complementary responsibilities.

--- Creative DNA ---
Reusable Creative DNA (mechanism and production grammar only):
Establish two distinct characters through separate, observable responsibilities before they interact. Give each one stable identity, tool, local action and intermediate result; only after both roles are independently legible may they meet at one shared interface where both contributions are required. End on a concrete joint result that could not exist if either role were removed, while preserving both identities.
Invariants:
- inv-01: Establish Character A through one stable tool, local operation and visible intermediate result.
- inv-02: Keep Character A's identity and result legible while its local operation completes.
- inv-03: Establish Character B later through a different tool, operation and intermediate result rather than replaying Character A.
- inv-04: Bring both roles to one shared interface where each contribution is visibly required.
- inv-05: Hold a concrete joint result while preserving both identities, tools and complementary responsibilities.
Variable slots:
- lead_subject: An original stable subject or pair whose role is readable without source identity.
- setting: A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface.
- carrier_or_token: One visible material, prop or symbolic anchor that carries the causal mechanism.
- state_change: A sequence of visible, causally ordered changes rather than decorative montage.
- camera_grammar: An information order that proves each change without copying source shot boundaries.
- payoff: A held final state that resolves or recontextualizes the setup.
- sound_family: An original hierarchy of visible-action sounds; no unsupported source-audio claim.
Failure prevention:
- A generic portrait provides no role for the second character to depend on.: Establish Character A through one stable tool, local operation and visible intermediate result.
- A prop held without consequence is only costume.: Keep Character A's identity and result legible while its local operation completes.
- An identical second introduction feels like duplicated character art.: Establish Character B later through a different tool, operation and intermediate result rather than replaying Character A.
- Placing characters side by side does not explain how they connect.: Bring both roles to one shared interface where each contribution is visibly required.
- A generic team pose or merged emblem cannot prove that both operations mattered.: Hold a concrete joint result while preserving both identities, tools and complementary responsibilities.
